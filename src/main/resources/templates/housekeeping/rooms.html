Housekeeping Standards & SOP Protocols

Operational benchmarks, inspection checklists, and service level agreements

Skiper103 Bouncy Accordion
Turnover Mandates:
  • Full linen replacement using 400-thread Egyptian cotton with hotel envelope folds.
  • Smart climate preset locked to 21°C with ambient air scent diffuser refreshed.
  • Replenish premium Acqua di Parma amenities and curated artisanal welcome fruits.
SLA & Verification:
  • Maximum turnaround time: 30 minutes from checkout notification.
  • Floor Supervisor must perform physical white-glove inspection before state changes to Inspected.
High-Touch Sterilization Points:

Mandatory 60-second contact time with quaternary ammonium disinfectant on door handles, TV remote controls, telephone handsets, light switches, and thermostat dials.

Airflow & Filtration:

Continuous vacuuming with certified HEPA filtration traps 99.97% of airborne microparticulates. Bathrooms undergo automatic ozone descaling treatment.

Zone 1: Foyer & Closet Hanger spacing equal, safe door open with reset code, laundry bag & shoehorn in place.
Zone 2: Bathroom Suite Marble chrome polished, mirrors streak-free, toilet seal tape attached, towels folded triple-stack.
Zone 3: Bedroom Pillow plumping 45-degree angle, bed skirt aligned, under-bed inspection negative for debris.

Encountered an issue during cleaning? Critical defects (leaks, AC failures, lock issues) trigger priority dispatch directly to engineering personnel.

Room status is immediately locked until engineering resolution.
